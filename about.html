HERITAGE RECIPES OF MEHERPUR & PURE ARTISANAL CARE

We believe true Bengal Achar isn't engineered with factory chemicals—it is slow-nurtured through open sunlight, cold-pressed wooden ghani mustard oil, and deep artisanal devotion.

ORIGIN • DARIYAPUR, MEHERPUR

FROM MUJIBNAGAR SOIL TO YOUR TABLE

Meherpur district in southwestern Bangladesh is celebrated across Bengal for its rich fertile loam and exceptional orchard fruit harvests. Our small-batch curing workshop is located in Dariapur, Mujibnagar.

Here, we harvest crisp green mangoes, plump olives, tart starfruit, wild hog plums, and fiery Naga and Bombay chilies directly from local partner orchards. Chemical ripening agents and industrial acids are strictly barred from our kitchens.

OUR 4 UNCOMPROMISING COMMITMENTS

01. COLD-PRESSED OIL

Cold-extracted from native brown mustard seeds using traditional wooden ghanis, providing authentic pungency and natural preservation.

02. NATURAL SUN-CURING

Never baked in industrial ovens. Every batch is cured gradually under open skies, allowing spices and mustard oil to infuse deep into the fruit fiber.

03. ZERO SYNTHETIC CHEMICALS

We never touch industrial acetic acid (synthetic vinegar) or chemical preservatives. Pure mustard oil and natural spices are nature's protectors.

04. BSTI CERTIFIED

Fully certified and licensed by the Bangladesh Standards & Testing Institution (BSTI). Official License No: BDS:CAC-260.
