Do your Achar contain synthetic vinegar or chemical preservatives? ↓
No. Bornobazar Achar is completely free of synthetic acetic acid (chemical vinegar) and synthetic food preservatives. Pure cold-pressed mustard oil and intense natural Meherpur sunshine serve as our natural preserving agents.
What is the shelf life and how should I store the Achar? ↓
Our Achar remains fresh and robust at room temperature for over 12 months. Refrigeration is never required. Always use a clean, dry spoon and keep a thin protective layer of mustard oil over the fruits.
