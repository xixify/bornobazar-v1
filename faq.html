FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about our handcrafted sun-curing process, natural ingredients, storage guidelines, and nationwide delivery.

1 Do you use chemicals, artificial vinegar, or preservatives in your Achar?
Absolutely not. Bornobazar never uses synthetic acetic acid (artificial vinegar) or industrial chemical preservatives. Pure first-press cold mustard oil from wooden ghanis and natural sunlight under Meherpur skies serve as our natural curing agents.
2 How long does the Achar stay fresh, and how should it be stored?
Our Achar remains fresh and aromatic at room temperature for over 12 months. No refrigeration is required. Always use a clean, dry spoon and ensure fruit pieces remain submerged under a protective layer of mustard oil. Sunning the jar occasionally keeps it in peak condition.
3 What are your delivery fees and transit times?
Inside Dhaka City delivery is ৳70 (home delivery within 24-48 hours). Outside Dhaka across all 64 districts courier delivery is ৳130 (2-4 business days). Delivery is 100% FREE on all orders of ৳999 or more!
4 Can I pay cash upon delivery (Cash on Delivery)?
Yes! We offer doorstep Cash on Delivery across all 64 districts, upazilas, and towns throughout Bangladesh. Inspect your parcel upon arrival and hand over payment.
5 What happens if a glass jar is damaged during transit?
Every glass jar is securely protected in multi-layer bubble wrap inside heavy corrugated boxes. In the rare event of transit damage, simply inspect the box upon delivery and notify us for an immediate free replacement parcel or complete refund.
6 Do you have official government certification or licenses?
Yes, Bornobazar is licensed and certified by the Bangladesh Standards & Testing Institution (BSTI). Official BSTI License No: BDS:CAC-260.
